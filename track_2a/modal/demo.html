Check a claim against a Swiss voting booklet

Pick an official booklet (German, French or Italian) and write a claim in any of the three languages. Apertus-v1.5-8B reads the 16 passages closest to the claim and says whether the booklet supports it, contradicts it or doesn't settle it, with the passages it relied on. This is the exact setup that scored macro-F1 0.891 on 976 held-out claims. It judges what the booklet says, not whether the claim is true.

Try one:
Apertus runs on one GPU that switches off when nobody uses it; the first claim after a pause waits for it to start (about 5 minutes). Limited to 30 claims per person per day. Claims you enter are sent to the model and not stored. Code, report and all 976 recorded test answers.
